Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop website composition for a Turkish wedding memory-services brand
Primary request: Design a shippable 1440x900 homepage first viewport and the beginning of the next section for “Konsept Gelsin”, a catalogue of six wedding memory experiences with WhatsApp as the only primary conversion.
Surface structure: a left vertical navigation rail on ivory paper with the wordmark, three anchors and WhatsApp; the rest is a dramatic horizontal flipbook stage. A massive typographic headline “Gece biter. Anılar konuşmaya devam eder.” occupies the upper left of the stage; below it one solid CTA. Six paper frames overlap in a controlled fan from left to right, each showing one distinct service artifact: QR album, Polaroid, video booth, interview mic, live broadcast, audio telephone. A single orange crank line passes through every frame and becomes the section divider. Show a glimpse of the next section as two large editorial service stories, not six repeated cards.
Visual world: modern cut-paper automata and photographic contact-sheet rhythm fused into one coherent system; precise folds, pinned joints, registration marks, paper texture, premium studio craft.
Color palette: ivory #FFF8EE base, large mandarin #FF5A1F field, deep ink #241B17, warm yellow #FFC44D, pale sage #D9E6CF.
Typography: bold experimental grotesk for display with Turkish characters, legible humanist sans for interface; no calligraphy or wedding serif.
Composition: asymmetric but trustworthy, bold scale, excellent whitespace, practical clickable controls and web proportions.
Text constraints: only exact text: “Konsept Gelsin”, “6 hizmet · tek güçlü hatıra”, “Gece biter. Anılar konuşmaya devam eder.”, “Hizmetleri keşfet”, “WhatsApp’tan yaz”, plus six service names where useful. No prices, testimonials, ratings, statistics, discounts, fake urgency, or badges.
Avoid: theme-template look, pink romance, black-and-gold luxury, gradients, glassmorphism, dashboard, mobile phone frame, equal cards everywhere, stock couple photo, watermark.
