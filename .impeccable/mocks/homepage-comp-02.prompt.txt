Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop website composition for a Turkish wedding memory-services brand
Primary request: Design a shippable 1440x900 homepage first viewport and the beginning of the next section for “Konsept Gelsin”. The page introduces six real wedding memory services and converts to WhatsApp.
Surface structure: ivory navigation across the top; an orange full-width hero staged like an open mechanical flipbook viewed almost front-on; the central flipbook is the dominant focal object and contains layered cut-paper representations of QR card, Polaroid, video, microphone, live camera, and retro telephone. Place the large headline “Gece biter. Anılar konuşmaya devam eder.” above and to the left of the mechanism without covering it. Place a high-contrast “WhatsApp’tan yaz” action directly beside the headline. A horizontal paper crank rule beneath the mechanism labels all six services and leads visibly into the next catalogue section, which uses alternating wide editorial rows rather than a card grid.
Visual world: precise kinetic paper engineering, visible pins and slots, folded labels, clean die-cut shapes, controlled realistic paper shadows and grain; modern and premium, not childlike.
Color palette: mandarin #FF5A1F owns roughly half the viewport, ivory #FFF8EE, ink #241B17, yellow #FFC44D, sage #D9E6CF.
Typography: characterful bold grotesk display and calm humanist sans body, full Turkish support; no serif, no script.
Motion implication: one hand-crank line would advance the six service scenes; the still comp must make this interaction obvious without extra instructional chrome.
Text constraints: exact text only: “Konsept Gelsin”, “6 hizmet · tek güçlü hatıra”, “Gece biter. Anılar konuşmaya devam eder.”, “Hizmetleri keşfet”, “WhatsApp’tan yaz”, and the six service names. No prices, discounts, ratings, testimonials, fake stats, or invented claims.
Avoid: phone mockup, black cards, ecommerce price tiles, pink, gradients, glass, bento grid, stock wedding photo, confetti, overly whimsical craft fair look, watermark.
